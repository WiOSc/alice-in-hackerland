Alice in Hackerland

Alice in Hackerland is VIT-AP's flagship hackathon at V-TAPP 2026, running 11–12 September at VIT-AP University, Amaravati.

Squads of two to three work through a series of game-based rounds that get harder as the day goes on, drawing on the same puzzle-box tension as the show it's named after.

Expect hidden "mystery" APIs to reverse-engineer on the fly, tight time pressure, and challenges that reward fast integration work as much as clean code.

Entry is ₹200 per team, with a ₹25,000 prize pool on the line for whichever team is still standing at the end.

External participants get accommodation arranged — full logistics, timings, and registration live on the official event page.
